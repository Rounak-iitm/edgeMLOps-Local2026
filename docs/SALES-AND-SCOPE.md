# Product Scope

EdgeMLOps Local is an on-premise MLOps runtime for machine-learning workloads at industrial sites. It provides local drift detection, retraining, ONNX validation, versioned model publication, and low-footprint inference.

It is not a safety PLC, SIS, MES, SCADA replacement, or a universal RUL guarantee. Industrial protocol integration and model acceptance are deployment services that must be validated per customer.

Recommended commercial offer:
- Site license: one production plant/edge environment.
- Enterprise license: multiple approved sites.
- Professional services: data/PLC integration, model validation and commissioning.
- Annual support/update contract.

Final pricing, warranty, liability, SLA, data-processing terms and redistribution rights must be supplied by the vendor's legal/commercial team.
